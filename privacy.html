Privacy Policy

Rezcute Studio · last updated November 20, 2026

Rezcute Studio only connects to the Rezcute.tv TikTok account. When authorized, it receives an access token and basic account information (open id, display name) from TikTok, used solely to upload videos as drafts and to read that account's own video statistics.

Tokens are stored privately on the Rezcute.tv operator's computer and are never sold or shared. The tool collects no data about other TikTok users or viewers. Revoking access in TikTok deletes our ability to use the token; on request we delete stored data within 30 days.
