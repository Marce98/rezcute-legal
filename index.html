Rezcute Studio

Rezcute Studio · last updated November 20, 2026

Rezcute Studio is the internal publishing tool of Rezcute.tv, a channel of short AI-made romantic micro-series. It sends each new episode to the Rezcute.tv TikTok account as a draft, where the account owner reviews and posts it. It is used only by the Rezcute.tv team and is not offered to other users.
